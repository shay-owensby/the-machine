# Context About Writer

Build a trustworthy, reusable business-context brief by interviewing the user. Optimize for completeness, specificity, and factual accuracy rather than speed.

## Ownership Boundary

`about.md` owns what is true about the business: identity, offers, audiences, positioning, proof, operations, policies, approved factual claims, and the action a reader should take. `soul.md` owns how the brand sounds: voice, tone, diction, rhythm, stylistic guardrails, and examples.

Do not interview for or store voice attributes, formality, humor, writing mechanics, signature phrases, stylistic preferences, or writing samples in `about.md`. When those details arise, preserve them for the Soul interview instead of expanding them here. Official names, exact product terminology, legally required language, prohibited factual claims, and CTA destinations remain business facts in `about.md`; `soul.md` may govern how they are expressed without copying them.

## Interview Method

1. Determine whether this is a new profile, a refresh, or gap-filling. If a prior brief is available, preserve confirmed facts and focus on missing or stale information.
2. Briefly explain that the interview will proceed in small groups and can be paused or resumed.
3. Ask one coherent topic cluster at a time, normally one to three questions per message. Begin with identity, offer, and ideal customer; adapt later questions to earlier answers.
4. Treat “relentless” as thorough, not coercive. Pursue vague, partial, inconsistent, or overly broad answers with concrete follow-ups, while always allowing the user to say “unknown,” “not applicable,” “skip,” or “come back later.”
5. Prefer questions that elicit usable facts. Ask for examples, ranges, boundaries, exceptions, and exact wording when they materially affect future writing.
6. Periodically summarize what has been learned, identify uncertain or conflicting facts, and ask the user to correct them.
7. Continue until every core category in [the interview guide](references/interview-guide.md) is answered, marked unknown, marked not applicable, or intentionally skipped. Cover extended categories when they would improve the requested writing context.
8. Finish with a final accuracy check and produce the brief described below. For a project context file, propose `./_context/ABOUT.md`; ask before writing or exporting anywhere not already authorized.

Do not dump the whole question bank at once. Do not repeat answered questions. Do not infer facts from silence, and do not turn guesses into business claims. Label assumptions, estimates, user opinions, and facts awaiting verification.

## Research and Sensitive Information

The user is the primary source. If public research would help, ask before using it unless the user already requested research. Cite researched facts and let the user correct them.

Do not request passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, private customer data, or other secrets. Ask only for business information useful to the brief. When financial, legal, health, or regulated claims arise, capture the user's wording and verification status without presenting them as independently validated.

## Deliverable

Produce a concise, skimmable source of truth with:

- business identity and overview;
- offers, pricing approach, purchasing process, and exclusions;
- ideal and non-ideal customers, buyer roles, needs, objections, and decision triggers;
- locations, service areas, delivery model, hours, time zone, and availability exceptions;
- positioning, differentiators, competitors or alternatives, proof, and approved claims;
- official names and factual terminology, message priorities, conversion actions and destinations, and topics or claims restricted for business, legal, or compliance reasons;
- contact, booking, operational, policy, and compliance details relevant to public writing;
- open questions, contradictions, stale facts, and items needing verification;
- a short “writer quick start” summarizing the audience, offer, promise, proof, and next action, with a direction to consult `soul.md` for voice and style.

Record a “last confirmed” date and identify the user as the source unless another source was used. Keep unknown fields visible so future sessions can resume efficiently.
